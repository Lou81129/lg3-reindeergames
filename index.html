The Tradition

Once a year, LG3 members trade their dignity for reindeer antlers and a little holiday cheer. It started last year. That makes it a tradition. We don't make the rules, we just print them.

Expect festive golf, questionable decisions, and lies that were bad long before you got to them. Seven tee times, seven foursomes, one trophy-adjacent prize. Until then, stretch, hydrate, and lower your expectations.

How to Play

  • Each hole is themed after one of Santa’s reindeer or holiday motifs.
  • Follow the scoring twist for that hole.
  • Keep track of points on the scorecard provided.
  • Highest team total at the end wins! Prizes for first and second place, plus a small tee gift.

The twists themselves are a surprise. You'll find out on the tee, and so will everyone else.
